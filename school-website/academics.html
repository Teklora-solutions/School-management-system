Our Programs

Three Stages, One Journey

Each program builds on the last — with specialist teachers, age-appropriate challenge, and a focus on the whole child.

🏫

Play-Based Learning

Structured play develops language, motor skills, and social-emotional foundations in our youngest learners.

🎥

Creative Arts

Music, art, and storytelling every day — because imagination is the first step to understanding.

🧒

Outdoor Discovery

Our garden and outdoor classroom give children space to explore nature, take safe risks, and build resilience.

📚

Strong Foundations

Literacy, numeracy, and inquiry-based science lay the groundwork for confident, independent learners.

🧠

Specialist Subjects

French, ICT, music, and PE are taught by specialist teachers from Grade 1 onwards.

🏅

Sports & Clubs

Football, swimming, chess, coding, drama — every child finds something they love after school.

🔬

Exam-Track Academics

Rigorous preparation for BECE and WASSCE with small classes, specialist labs, and targeted support.

💻

Technology & Innovation

Robotics, coding, and a makerspace prepare students for careers that don't exist yet.

🎓

University Guidance

One-on-one counselling, career fairs, and application support from Grade 10 through graduation.
