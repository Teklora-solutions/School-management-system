How to Apply

Four Simple Steps

From first inquiry to first day, we'll guide you through every step.

1

Submit Inquiry

Fill out the inquiry form below. Our admissions office will respond within 48 hours.

2

Campus Visit

Tour the campus, meet teachers, and see a class in session. We'll arrange a time that works for you.

3

Assessment

A friendly, age-appropriate assessment helps us understand your child's needs and place them in the right class.

4

Welcome

Receive your offer, complete enrolment, and join us for your first day at Springfield.
