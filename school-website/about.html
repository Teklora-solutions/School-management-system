About Springfield Academy

Forty Years of Believing in Children

Founded in 1985, Springfield Academy has grown from a single classroom of 12 students to a thriving community of 1,200 learners — all guided by the same belief: that every child deserves to be seen, challenged, and celebrated.

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, inquiry-driven education that empowers every child to think critically, act compassionately, and lead with confidence in a changing world.

👁

Our Vision

To be the school our community trusts to prepare young people who are academically strong, emotionally resilient, and ready to contribute meaningfully to society.

❤

Our Values

Curiosity, kindness, integrity, and excellence. These four words guide every decision we make, from the playground to the boardroom.

Our Journey

Four Decades of Growth

1985

The First Classroom

Springfield Academy opens its doors with 12 students and one teacher in a converted house on Education Road.

1995

Expanding the Campus

A new primary building and library are opened, growing enrolment to 300 students across eight grades.

2003

Secondary School Launches

Springfield adds a secondary program with science labs, a sports field, and specialist teachers.

2015

Green Campus Initiative

Solar panels, a student-led garden, and a zero-waste cafeteria make Springfield one of the region's most sustainable schools.

2025

1,200 Students Strong

Today, Springfield serves 1,200 students from Early Years through Secondary, with a 98% graduation rate and alumni in 30+ countries.

Principal's Welcome

A Message from Dr. Abena Owusu

Welcome to Springfield Academy. For forty years, we have believed that education is not just about exams — it is about character, curiosity, and community. When you walk through our gates, you will see children who are excited to learn, teachers who know each student by name, and a campus that feels like home.

I invite you to visit us, ask questions, and see for yourself what makes Springfield different. We would be honoured to partner with your family on this journey.

Dr. Abena Owusu
Principal, Springfield Academy
