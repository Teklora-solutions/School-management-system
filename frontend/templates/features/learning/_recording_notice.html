Recording notice. Some lessons may be recorded so that pupils who miss class can catch up. The teacher will say at the start if a lesson is being recorded, and only the teacher can start it. If you would rather your child's face or voice were not in the recording, ask them to keep their camera off — they can still follow the lesson and use the chat. The school shares recordings with this class only and removes them at the end of the term.
