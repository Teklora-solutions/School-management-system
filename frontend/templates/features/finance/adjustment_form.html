What happens when you save

  • A discount, scholarship, waiver or other reduction posts straight away: the debt drops and the ledger records it against Discounts, which is contra income — money the school gave up, not a cost it paid out.
  • A write-off — bad debt, a decision to stop chasing — is saved as a request. It changes nothing until an Admin posts it, so an outstanding figure cannot quietly disappear on one signature.
  • A pending write-off still shows on the pupil's statement, marked Awaiting approval, and is not taken off the balance.
  • Pick an invoice to reduce that bill. Leaving it blank adjusts the pupil's general account instead.
  • Nothing here can be edited afterwards — a mistake is answered with a new adjustment, and both stay visible.
