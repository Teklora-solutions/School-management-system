What happens when you save

  • The books take Debit the expense category and Credit Petty cash or Bank, matching which side you paid from.
  • A claim over the school's approval limit, recorded by someone who is not an Admin, is saved as Awaiting approval and posts no ledger line at all until an Admin signs it. The officer who spends is not the officer who authorises.
  • Each posted claim takes the next expense reference from the gap-free sequence, so an auditor can see nothing is missing.
  • A claim cannot be edited once posted. A mistake is answered with a new entry, and both stay visible.
  • Fees are never collected through this page — use Take Payment so the money lands against a pupil's bill.
