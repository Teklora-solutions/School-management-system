What happens when you save

  • A receipt number is taken from this year’s sequence, so none is ever reused or skipped twice.
  • The posting is DR the settling account / CR Fees Receivable, and the settling account is stored on the receipt rather than re-derived later.
  • Money is applied to the pupil’s oldest open invoice first — this term’s and earlier terms’ alike. An overpayment is not a note: it posts to Student Credit Balances and is spent on the next invoice.
  • A reference that already exists is refused, and submitting this form twice does not post twice.
  • A reversal needs a reason of at least 10 characters. Above the school’s threshold it is a second signature away, not a click.
