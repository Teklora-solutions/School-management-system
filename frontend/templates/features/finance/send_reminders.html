Who actually receives it

Pressing Publish creates one announcement in Communications, addressed by role to students and parents. Every family therefore sees the same notice, including any whose fees are already clear.

Naming specific pupils in a reminder is not wired up yet: the list beside this box is who to chase, not who will be contacted. Write the notice so it reads as a general call, and use the export for individual follow-up.
Announcements are the school's own noticeboard — no email or SMS leaves the system from this page.
