Community rules

  1. Be kind and respectful. Treat everyone with the same courtesy you would expect in person.
  2. Keep it relevant. Post in the right category and stay on topic.
  3. Protect privacy. Never share another person's personal information.
  4. No spam or advertising. Promotional content not authorised by the school will be removed.
  5. Safeguarding first. Report any content that concerns you to the school admin immediately.
  6. What you post represents you. Posts are visible to the audience of their category — think before you post.
