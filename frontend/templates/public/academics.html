Our Programmes

Three Stages, One Journey

Each programme builds on the last — with specialist teachers, age-appropriate challenge and a focus on the whole child.

Play-Based Learning

Structured play develops language, motor skills and social-emotional foundations.

Creative Arts

Music, art and storytelling every day — imagination is the first step to understanding.

Outdoor Discovery

Our garden and outdoor classroom give children space to explore nature and build resilience.

Strong Foundations

Literacy, numeracy and inquiry-based science lay the groundwork for independent learners.

Specialist Subjects

French, ICT, music and PE are taught by specialist teachers from Grade 1 onwards.

Sports & Clubs

Football, swimming, chess, coding, drama — every child finds something they love.

Exam-Track Academics

Rigorous preparation for BECE and WASSCE with small classes and specialist labs.

Technology & Innovation

Robotics, coding and a makerspace prepare students for careers that don't exist yet.

University Guidance

One-on-one counselling, career fairs and application support from Grade 10.
